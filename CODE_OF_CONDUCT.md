# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone who participates in this project.

We pledge to treat all participants with respect, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

We strive to create a community where everyone can contribute, learn, collaborate, and share ideas without fear of harassment or discrimination.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of others.
- Welcoming different perspectives and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on collaboration and the best interests of the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, derogatory, or offensive comments.
- Unwanted sexual attention or advances.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or community activities.
- Any other conduct that is inappropriate in a collaborative environment.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers and community moderators are responsible for maintaining a respectful environment and addressing violations of this Code of Conduct.

They may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, issues, or other content that violates these standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related spaces, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, communication channels, and events.

It also applies when individuals represent the project in public or professional settings.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it to the project maintainers through an appropriate private communication channel.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with reasonable care to protect the privacy of those involved.

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity and frequency of a violation, possible actions include:

1. **Warning:** A private notice explaining the violation and requesting corrective behavior.
2. **Temporary Restriction:** Temporary suspension of participation in project activities.
3. **Permanent Restriction:** Removal of participation privileges for serious or repeated violations.

Enforcement decisions should be fair, proportionate, and focused on maintaining a safe and respectful community environment.
