Platform Capabilities

Everything You Need to Succeed Academically

Purpose-built scholastic tools engineered to support curriculum delivery, timely assignment submissions, fair evaluation, and balanced study planning.

Course & Learning Materials

Browse syllabus modules, access lecturer lecture slides, spreadsheets, and reference reading materials with secure, permission-guarded downloads.

Assignments & Submissions

View instructions and effort estimates, submit coursework within active deadline windows, receive lecturer evaluations, and track grading progress.

Quizzes & Academic Results

Take timed multiple-choice and short-answer quizzes with server-side time-limit enforcement, automated grading, manual scoring desks, and detailed score slips.

Smart Workload Planner

A deterministic, rule-based academic planner that prioritizes pending tasks, identifies deadline clashes, classifies workload levels, and generates personalized 7-day study pacing.

Academic Calendar

Consolidated scholastic timeline featuring semester terms, institutional milestones, exam dates, assignment deadlines, and quiz availability windows in Month, Week, or List views.

Role-Based Governance

Dedicated command centers for Administrators, Lecturers, Students, and Parents with strict server-side scoping and IDOR-safe access controls on every endpoint.
